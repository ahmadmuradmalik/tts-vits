Types of Text to speech models currently

1. Autoregressive Models
Tacotron / Tacotron 2

Sequence-to-sequence with attention.

Input: phonemes or character embeddings.

Output: mel-spectrogram.

Uses a vocoder (e.g., Griffin-Lim, WaveNet) to synthesize final waveform.

Loss: MSE on spectrogram + guided attention loss.

Strength: high-quality prosody.

Weakness: slow inference, exposure bias.

2. Non-Autoregressive / Parallel Models
FastSpeech / FastSpeech 2

Transformer-based model that predicts durations to remove autoregressive bottleneck.

Input: text/phonemes + duration, pitch, energy predictors.

Output: mel-spectrogram in parallel.

Loss: MSE on mel-spectrogram + duration/pitch loss.

Faster, more stable.

3. End-to-End Models
VITS (Variational Inference TTS)

Combines variational autoencoders (VAE), normalizing flows, and GANs.

Learns to directly synthesize waveform from text.

Uses adversarial loss (GAN) and reconstruction loss.

No intermediate spectrogram step required.

State-of-the-art realism and speed.
